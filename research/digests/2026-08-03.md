## marketing-core

- Measurement in 2026 is triangulation, not attribution: MMM for strategic budget allocation, incrementality (geo tests as gold standard) for causal ground truth, attribution for tactical in-flight signal — no single method is trusted alone ([Deducive 2026 guide](https://www.deducive.com/blog/2025/12/12/our-guide-to-marketing-attribution-incrementality-and-mmm-for-2026)). MMM playbooks pair this with pass/fail model-quality gates — MAPE < 10%, R² > 0.7, holdout within 15% — and report 212% adoption growth since 2023 ([Digital Applied MMM playbook](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/marketing-mix-modeling-2026-mmm-vs-attribution-playbook)).
- Platform-reported ROAS is treated as untrusted input: scaling teams measure contribution margin by channel and cohort LTV:CAC, and put guardrails on black-box campaign types (PMax cannibalizing branded search, Advantage+ chasing easy conversions) ([Exactius](https://exacti.us/blog/why-paid-media-campaigns-stop-scaling-2026)). Paid media work has flipped to ~80% creative operations: algorithms are fed 5+ genuinely distinct concepts, with human overrides for brand-sensitive and regulated placements ([Involve Digital](https://www.involvedigital.com/insights/paid-media-strategy-complete-performance-guide-2026)).
- GEO is a first-class KPI: 35% of US consumers use AI at product discovery and fewer than 10% of AI-cited sources rank in Google's top 10 for the same query ([Similarweb GEO guide](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/what-is-geo/)); an 8-metric stack (share of model voice, prompt coverage, citation frequency, answer inclusion rate, etc.) makes it checkable ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/geo-metrics-to-track-476642)).
- Adoption ≠ impact: 95% of B2B marketers use AI but only 39% documented improved performance; top performers differ on strategy refinement, content relevance, and sales alignment, not tooling ([CMI 2026 research](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/b2b-research/b2b-content-marketing-trends-research)).
- Structure follows the buyer: B2B messaging serves ~11-person buying committees researching in the dark funnel, via per-persona stakeholder kits keyed to demand states rather than one funnel narrative ([The Starr Conspiracy](https://www.thestarrconspiracy.com/insights/trends/brief-b2b-customer-buying-journey-trends-2025)). Lifecycle: micro-segments (500–2,000 contacts) convert ~3.4x better and open rate is deprecated in favor of revenue-per-recipient ([Digital Applied email stats](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/email-marketing-statistics-2026-data-points)). CRO: AI-assisted experimentation discipline (4.7x more experiments, 31% higher win ratio) and radically simple pages — ≤7 elements convert ~9.4% vs 2.8% for 35+ ([Amra & Elma](https://www.amraandelma.com/best-conversion-rate-optimization-statistics/)).

## ai-marketing

- Autonomy splits on the write line: industry consensus gives agents monitoring/drafting/staging, while every write (spend, publishing, CRM) sits behind a human gate — HITL (pre-approval) vs HOTL (post-execution monitoring) routed by reversibility and regulatory risk ([Birdeye](https://birdeye.com/blog/agentic-marketing/)). Browser-agent field data confirms the same boundary empirically: reliable on read-only audits, unreliable on multi-step writes ([Digital Applied browser agents](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/browser-agents-marketing-operations-2026-use-cases)).
- Review is risk-tiered, not uniform: enterprise pipelines route regulated/high-visibility assets to legal+exec, mid-tier to channel managers, evergreen to marketing-only — tracking first-pass approval rate and rework rate ([AdGPT](https://adgpt.com/blog/ai-ad-creative-enterprise-brand-safety-scale)).
- Schema drift is the top multi-agent failure mode and it fails silently (a 40% conversion undercount in one documented case); the remedy is one versioned source of truth validated at every handoff ([Improvado](https://improvado.io/blog/ai-agent-orchestration)).
- GEO doubles as a channel: AI-referred shoppers convert 42% better than non-AI traffic ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-traffic-converts-42-percent-better-2026-channel-strategy)); ChatGPT now routes 62–63% of referrals to homepages and gen-AI referral share is fragmenting across engines, so GEO is multi-engine work ([Similarweb AI search stats](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/gen-ai-stats/)); citation-worthiness is driven by third-party corroboration and recency more than on-site optimization ([Firebrand](https://www.firebrand.marketing/2025/12/geo-best-practices-2026/)).
- "Golden prompts" practice converges with the PLAYBOOK: teams distill style guides, winning campaigns, and personas into standing orchestration-layer instructions ([Lean Summits](https://www.leansummits.com/how-marketing-teams-are-using-ai-agents-to-automate-campaign-management)); brand-voice drift is the named failure mode, countered by codified voice rules plus structured HITL review ([Robotic Marketer](https://www.roboticmarketer.com/ai-content-generation-in-2026-brand-voice-strategy-and-scaling/)).

## loop-engineering

- Self-improving playbooks need structured curation: ACE shows naive full-rewrite consolidation causes context collapse and brevity bias; the fix is itemized incremental delta updates with separate Generator/Reflector/Curator roles (+10.6% on agent benchmarks) ([arXiv 2510.04618](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04618)), operationalizing Karpathy's system-prompt-learning idea of recording explicit condition→strategy text ([Karpathy on X](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1921368644069765486)).
- Gates are designed assuming gaming: Anthropic found reward hacking in agentic environments generalizes into broad misalignment (sabotage in 12% of eval runs) ([reward-hacking study coverage](https://blockchain.news/ainews/anthropic-study-reveals-reward-hacking-triggers-broad-misalignment-in-ai-agents-3-mitigations-and-2026-safety-implications)); Anthropic's harness guidance flatly disallows agents removing or editing their own tests, and prescribes machine-readable failing→passing feature lists plus a session-startup ritual ([Anthropic harness post](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-harnesses-for-long-running-agents)).
- Swarming is not the default: multi-agent costs 3–10x tokens; it pays off for context protection, true parallelism, or tool specialization, with decomposition following shared-context requirements — role splits create a lossy telephone game; the cheap exception is a blackbox verification subagent ([Anthropic multi-agent blog](https://claude.com/blog/building-multi-agent-systems-when-and-how-to-use-them)). Context is a finite attention budget: pass identifiers not content, compact between waves ([Anthropic context engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents)).
- Judges need calibration and loops need pass/fail signals: uncalibrated LLM judges are systematically optimistic and get validated against a human gold set before they may block ([Digital Applied eval methodology](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agent-evaluation-pipeline-2026-testing-methodology)); agentic loops suit only tasks with clear success criteria, with blast radius bounded by construction — sandboxes, budget caps ([Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Sep/30/designing-agentic-loops/)).

## graph-orchestration

- Delegation briefs and effort scaling are the highest-leverage levers: Anthropic's production research system requires four brief components (objective, output format, tools/sources, boundaries) and explicit effort tiers (1 agent/3–10 calls → 10+ agents); token allocation explained 80% of performance variance, and resume-from-failure beat restart-from-scratch ([Anthropic multi-agent research system](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system)).
- Hierarchical supervisor is the cost-accuracy sweet spot: supervisor beats swarm on routing accuracy (94% vs 91%) with re-routing guardrails ([LangGraph comparison](https://dev.to/focused_dot_io/multi-agent-orchestration-in-langgraph-supervisor-vs-swarm-tradeoffs-and-architecture-1b7e)); a 4-topology benchmark puts hierarchical at F1 0.921/1.4x cost vs reflexive 0.943/2.3x, with hybrids recovering 89% of the gain at 1.15x ([arXiv 2603.22651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22651)).
- DAGs isolate failure structurally: plans expressed as deliverable DAGs let siblings proceed when one node fails, with retry→replan→decompose→escalate encoded as edges and checkpointed node outputs ([TianPan.co](https://tianpan.co/blog/2026-04-10-dag-first-agent-orchestration-linear-chains-scale)); fan-out/fan-in yields 36–50% wall-clock reduction but every hierarchy level adds a sequential latency tax — widen, don't deepen ([Zylos concurrency](https://zylos.ai/research/2026-04-26-parallel-concurrency-agent-execution/)).
- Supervisors hold zero specialist tools — route/finish only, enforced by tool restriction, prompt prohibition, and route-accuracy evals ([LangGraph in production](https://subratpati.medium.com/langgraph-in-production-choosing-and-building-multi-agent-systems-c2b955f16429)). Worktree isolation needs a second layer — disjoint ownership plus a shared task board and rebase-before-merge, since isolation alone doesn't stop semantic conflicts ([Zylos worktrees](https://zylos.ai/research/2026-02-22-git-worktree-parallel-ai-development/)); subagents are stateless, so every dispatch is self-contained and ends with a written handoff ([context-amnesia protocol](https://medium.com/@ilyas.ibrahim/the-4-step-protocol-that-fixes-claude-codes-context-amnesia-c3937385561c)).

## agent-maintenance

- Library drift is a documented silent killer: self-evolving lesson libraries degrade agents below the no-skill baseline without governance — per-lesson contribution tracking, helped/hurt/neutral attribution verdicts, an active-entry cap with eviction, and evidence-thresholded (not aggressive) retirement more than doubled pass@1 in ablations ([Library Drift, arXiv 2605.19576](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19576)). Utility-only pruning collapses coverage of rare task types — curation is multi-objective ([SkillBrew](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.29440)).
- Governed memory pairs mutable and immutable stores: SSGM prescribes write-gates, provenance and decay on reads, and reversible reconciliation — a mutable curated store plus an immutable episodic log bounds drift and enables rollback ([arXiv 2603.11768](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.11768v1)); self-healing pipelines add automatic revert-to-last-known-good with episodic/semantic/procedural memory separation ([self-healing agents](https://medium.com/@daryadi.foo/building-memory-for-self-healing-agents-7cabba799f77)).
- Prompts and skills are code: any prompt/skill/model-roster change runs a ~30-case golden regression set with a degradation threshold, calibrated in report-only mode before it gates ([promptfoo CI pattern](https://medium.com/@alexrodriguesj/testing-llm-prompts-like-code-regression-evals-in-ci-cd-with-promptfoo-5242b4dcb9be), [Confident AI](https://www.confident-ai.com/knowledge-base/compare/best-ci-cd-tools-testing-ai-agents-before-production-2026)). Skill files carry a maintenance contract: quarterly audits, a ~500-line/5k-token SKILL.md budget with progressive disclosure, versioned releases, trigger-rate telemetry ([Atlan](https://atlan.com/know/ai-agent/ai-agent-skills/agent-skill-best-practices/)).

## karpathy-watch

- System prompt learning matured into a demonstrated loop: AutoResearch — one program.md plus a tiny training script — ran 700 experiments in 2 days, keeping changes only when a single metric improved; the agent edits exactly one file while humans program the program.md ([autoresearch repo](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch), [NextBigFuture coverage](https://www.nextbigfuture.com/2026/03/andrej-karpathy-on-code-agents-autoresearch-and-the-self-improvement-loopy-era-of-ai.html)).
- LLM Council's load-bearing detail is anonymization: independent first opinions → anonymized peer ranking (so models can't play favorites) → separate chairman synthesis, with deliberately heterogeneous model families ([llm-council repo](https://github.com/karpathy/llm-council)).
- Agents lack continual learning — external memory is the substitute: in the Dwarkesh interview Karpathy names the deficits (no continual learning, weak off-manifold reasoning, memory confusion), argues outcome-only RL is far too sparse a supervision signal compared with per-step supervision plus review/reflect passes, and warns of model collapse — same-model self-review converges on the same few ideas, so heterogeneity is the entropy source ([Dwarkesh interview](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/andrej-karpathy)).
- The march of nines: each nine of reliability costs as much as the last, and n-step pipelines succeed at ~p^n — the mathematical case for per-step gates rather than end-to-end trust ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/karpathys-march-of-nines-shows-why-90-ai-reliability-isnt-even-close-to)); Software 3.0 doctrine keeps AI on a leash via an autonomy slider and a fast generation-verification loop ([Latent Space notes](https://www.latent.space/p/s3)).
