# Current practices

Verification-supported distillation only; the full weekly record lives in
`research/digests/`. Every claim carries at least one citation.

## marketing-core

- Measurement is triangulated across MMM (budget allocation), incrementality (causal ground truth, geo tests as gold standard), and attribution (in-flight signal), with no single method trusted alone ([Deducive](https://www.deducive.com/blog/2025/12/12/our-guide-to-marketing-attribution-incrementality-and-mmm-for-2026)); MMM quality is gated numerically — MAPE < 10%, R² > 0.7, holdout within 15% ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/marketing-mix-modeling-2026-mmm-vs-attribution-playbook)).
- Platform-reported ROAS is treated as untrusted input; scaling teams steer on contribution margin by channel and cohort LTV:CAC, with guardrails on black-box campaign types ([Exactius](https://exacti.us/blog/why-paid-media-campaigns-stop-scaling-2026)).
- Paid media is ~80% creative operations: algorithms are fed 5+ genuinely distinct concepts (not variants), with human overrides for brand-sensitive and regulated placements ([Involve Digital](https://www.involvedigital.com/insights/paid-media-strategy-complete-performance-guide-2026)).
- Lifecycle programs use micro-segments of 500–2,000 contacts (~3.4x conversion) and grade on revenue-per-recipient rather than open rate ([Digital Applied email stats](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/email-marketing-statistics-2026-data-points)); landing pages stay radically simple — ≤7 elements convert ~9.4% vs 2.8% for 35+ — under disciplined experimentation ([Amra & Elma](https://www.amraandelma.com/best-conversion-rate-optimization-statistics/)).
- B2B messaging serves ~11-person buying committees via per-persona stakeholder kits keyed to demand states, not one funnel narrative ([Starr Conspiracy](https://www.thestarrconspiracy.com/insights/trends/brief-b2b-customer-buying-journey-trends-2025)); AI adoption alone is not impact — 95% of B2B marketers use it, 39% documented improved performance ([CMI](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/b2b-research/b2b-content-marketing-trends-research)).
- GEO is a measurable KPI: 35% of US consumers use AI at product discovery, under 10% of AI-cited sources rank in Google's top 10 for the same query ([Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/what-is-geo/)), and an 8-metric stack (share of model voice, prompt coverage, citation frequency, answer inclusion rate) makes it checkable ([Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/geo-metrics-to-track-476642)).

## ai-marketing

- Agent autonomy splits on the write line: monitoring/drafting/staging run autonomously, while every write (spend, publishing, CRM) sits behind a human gate, with pre-approval vs post-hoc monitoring routed by reversibility and regulatory risk ([Birdeye](https://birdeye.com/blog/agentic-marketing/)); browser-agent field data shows the same boundary — reliable on read-only audits, unreliable on multi-step writes ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/browser-agents-marketing-operations-2026-use-cases)).
- Content review is risk-tiered: regulated/high-visibility assets route to legal+exec, mid-tier to channel managers, evergreen to marketing-only, tracked by first-pass approval and rework rates ([AdGPT](https://adgpt.com/blog/ai-ad-creative-enterprise-brand-safety-scale)).
- Schema drift is the top multi-agent data failure and it fails silently; the remedy is one versioned source of truth validated at every handoff ([Improvado](https://improvado.io/blog/ai-agent-orchestration)).
- AI-referred traffic is a distinct converting channel — 42% better conversion than non-AI traffic ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-traffic-converts-42-percent-better-2026-channel-strategy)) — with most AI referrals landing on homepages ([Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/blog/marketing/geo/gen-ai-stats/)), and citation-worthiness driven more by third-party corroboration and recency than on-site optimization ([Firebrand](https://www.firebrand.marketing/2025/12/geo-best-practices-2026/)).
- Teams codify brand voice, winning campaigns, and personas into standing orchestration-layer instructions ("golden prompts"), because brand-voice drift is the named failure mode of scaled AI content ([Lean Summits](https://www.leansummits.com/how-marketing-teams-are-using-ai-agents-to-automate-campaign-management), [Robotic Marketer](https://www.roboticmarketer.com/ai-content-generation-in-2026-brand-voice-strategy-and-scaling/)).

## loop-engineering

- Self-improving playbooks are curated as itemized incremental deltas with separate generator/reflector/curator roles; naive full-file rewrites cause context collapse and brevity bias ([ACE, arXiv 2510.04618](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04618)), matching the system-prompt-learning pattern of explicit condition→strategy entries ([Karpathy](https://x.com/karpathy/status/1921368644069765486)).
- Acceptance gates assume gaming: reward hacking in agentic environments generalizes into broader misalignment ([reward-hacking study](https://blockchain.news/ainews/anthropic-study-reveals-reward-hacking-triggers-broad-misalignment-in-ai-agents-3-mitigations-and-2026-safety-implications)), and harness guidance keeps tests outside agent write access with machine-readable pass/fail feature lists ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-harnesses-for-long-running-agents)).
- Multi-agent runs cost 3–10x tokens, so decomposition follows shared-context requirements rather than role labels, with the blackbox verification subagent as the cheap exception ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/building-multi-agent-systems-when-and-how-to-use-them)); context is a finite attention budget — pass identifiers not content, compact between waves ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents)).
- LLM judges are systematically optimistic until calibrated against a human gold set, and only then may they block ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/ai-agent-evaluation-pipeline-2026-testing-methodology)); agentic loops suit tasks with clear success criteria and a blast radius bounded by construction ([Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Sep/30/designing-agentic-loops/)).

## graph-orchestration

- Delegation briefs carry four mandatory components — objective, output format, tools/sources, boundaries — with explicit effort tiers per task, and resume-from-failure beats restart-from-scratch ([Anthropic multi-agent research system](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system)).
- Hierarchical supervision is the cost-accuracy sweet spot versus swarm and reflexive topologies ([LangGraph comparison](https://dev.to/focused_dot_io/multi-agent-orchestration-in-langgraph-supervisor-vs-swarm-tradeoffs-and-architecture-1b7e), [arXiv 2603.22651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22651)), and supervisors hold zero specialist tools — route/finish only ([LangGraph in production](https://subratpati.medium.com/langgraph-in-production-choosing-and-building-multi-agent-systems-c2b955f16429)).
- Plans expressed as deliverable DAGs isolate failure — siblings proceed when one node fails, recovery is encoded as edges ([TianPan.co](https://tianpan.co/blog/2026-04-10-dag-first-agent-orchestration-linear-chains-scale)); fan-out cuts wall-clock 36–50% but each hierarchy level adds sequential latency — widen, don't deepen ([Zylos](https://zylos.ai/research/2026-04-26-parallel-concurrency-agent-execution/)).
- Worktree isolation needs a second layer — disjoint file ownership, a shared board, rebase-before-merge — because isolation alone doesn't stop semantic conflicts ([Zylos](https://zylos.ai/research/2026-02-22-git-worktree-parallel-ai-development/)); subagents are stateless, so dispatches are self-contained and end with a written handoff ([context-amnesia protocol](https://medium.com/@ilyas.ibrahim/the-4-step-protocol-that-fixes-claude-codes-context-amnesia-c3937385561c)).

## agent-maintenance

- Ungoverned self-evolving lesson libraries degrade agents below the no-skill baseline; governance is per-lesson contribution tracking, helped/hurt/neutral verdicts, an active-entry cap with eviction, and evidence-thresholded retirement ([Library Drift, arXiv 2605.19576](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19576)).
- Pruning on utility alone collapses coverage of rare task types — library curation is multi-objective ([SkillBrew](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.29440)).
- Governed agent memory pairs a mutable curated store with an immutable episodic log, plus write-gates, provenance on reads, and reversible reconciliation for rollback ([SSGM, arXiv 2603.11768](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.11768v1)); self-healing pipelines add automatic revert-to-last-known-good ([self-healing agents](https://medium.com/@daryadi.foo/building-memory-for-self-healing-agents-7cabba799f77)).
- Prompt/skill/roster changes run a ~30-case golden regression set with a degradation threshold, calibrated in report-only mode before it gates ([promptfoo CI pattern](https://medium.com/@alexrodriguesj/testing-llm-prompts-like-code-regression-evals-in-ci-cd-with-promptfoo-5242b4dcb9be), [Confident AI](https://www.confident-ai.com/knowledge-base/compare/best-ci-cd-tools-testing-ai-agents-before-production-2026)); skill files carry a maintenance contract — quarterly audits, a size budget with progressive disclosure, versioned releases ([Atlan](https://atlan.com/know/ai-agent/ai-agent-skills/agent-skill-best-practices/)).

## karpathy-watch

- The self-improvement loop is demonstrated at minimal scale: AutoResearch edits exactly one program.md, keeping a change only when a single metric improves, across 700 experiments in 2 days ([autoresearch](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch), [coverage](https://www.nextbigfuture.com/2026/03/andrej-karpathy-on-code-agents-autoresearch-and-the-self-improvement-loopy-era-of-ai.html)).
- Council-style review works via anonymized peer ranking on defined axes with a separate chairman synthesis, using deliberately heterogeneous model families ([llm-council](https://github.com/karpathy/llm-council)).
- Models lack continual learning, so external curated memory substitutes for it; outcome-only RL is a sparse supervision signal versus per-step supervision with review/reflect passes; same-model self-review collapses onto the same few ideas — heterogeneity is the entropy source ([Dwarkesh interview](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/andrej-karpathy)).
- Reliability follows a march of nines — each nine costs as much as the last, and n-step pipelines succeed at ~p^n, the case for per-step gates over end-to-end trust ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/karpathys-march-of-nines-shows-why-90-ai-reliability-isnt-even-close-to)) — with autonomy kept on a slider and a fast generation-verification loop ([Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/s3)).
