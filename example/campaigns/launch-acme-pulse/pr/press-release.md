## Acme Analytics launches Acme Pulse, a product-analytics dashboard for teams that own activation

*(Headline is the best-of-N winner — see WALKTHROUGH.md Phase 2 for the two
alternatives and the adjudication.)*

**SAN FRANCISCO — June 22, 2026 —** Acme Analytics today announced the general
availability of **Acme Pulse**, a product-analytics dashboard that turns a company's
existing warehouse event data into funnels, retention curves, and activation reports
without requiring SQL or a separate data pipeline.

Acme Pulse connects to a team's existing warehouse — Postgres, BigQuery, or
Snowflake — rather than asking customers to copy data into a new store.[^src:warehouse-support]
In the company's onboarding study, new users built their first funnel in under 10
minutes without writing SQL.[^src:onboarding-study]

"Product managers shouldn't have to file a ticket and wait to see their own product
data," said Dana Rivera, VP of Product at Acme Analytics. "We built Acme Pulse so the
person who owns activation can answer their own questions the same day they ask them."

Acme Pulse is available today with a 14-day free trial that requires no credit
card.[^src:trial-terms] In the company's early trial cohort, 18% of free trials
converted to paid.[^src:trial-conversion]

### About Acme Analytics

Acme Analytics builds product-analytics tools for teams that own activation and
